# Cambridge Doctoral Training Program (DTP) Workshop
## AI for Research: Seed Prompt Repository
**Title:** How AI is reshaping research and the academy  
**Date:** Wednesday, 29 April 2026  
**Location:** The Pitt Building, University of Cambridge  
**Instructor:** Jacob Forward, Faculty of History  

Welcome to the practical session of this Cambridge DTP workshop. This repository contains "Seed Prompts" which are carefully engineered instructions designed to transform a standard Large Language Model (LLM) into a specialized interlocutor.

## Getting Started

To begin a session, follow these steps:

1.  **Select a Module:** Choose one of the four modules listed below.
2.  **Copy the Content:** Open the corresponding `.md` file in this folder, and select the **"Copy raw file"** option in GitHub.
3.  **Initiate the AI:** Open your preferred LLM (e.g., Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude) and paste the entire prompt into a **new chat**.
4.  **Engage in Dialogue:** The AI will introduce itself and initiate a guided 10 minute interview or consultation.

---

## Available Modules

Participants are encouraged to begin with **"About Skills"** to establish a foundational understanding before exploring the other specialized modules.

### 1. [About Skills](./About-Skills.md)
*Focus: Foundational knowledge of the Agent Skills framework.*
An interactive information session. The AI will consult official technical documentation to explain "Agent Skills" and how they extend LLM capabilities beyond simple prompting. This module covers advanced features such as executable scripts and domain-specific reference libraries.

### 2. [Skill Creator](./Skill-Creator.md)
*Focus: Automation and custom tool development.*
Collaborate with a "Skill Architect" to design a personalized `Skill.md` file. This interview assists you in translating repetitive research tasks into high-performance system prompts for consistent long-term use.

### 3. [AI Opportunity Scout](./AI-Opportunity-Scout.md)
*Focus: Strategic integration of AI in your research workflows.*
A strategy-focused session that frames AI as a "Translation Technology." Identify high-leverage points in your research for AI application and consider how processing data at scale may influence your core research questions.

### 4. [Socratic Tutor](./Socratic-Tutor.md)
*Focus: Critical interrogation of research challenges.*
A disciplined dialogue based on the classical Socratic Method (Elenchus). The AI acts as a "Midwife of Ideas," utilizing rigorous logic to help you interrogate complex challenges in your doctoral work. At the conclusion, it provides a persistent prompt for setting up a long-term personal Socratic tutor.

---

## Guidelines for Success

*   **Sequential Interaction:** These prompts are designed for iterative dialogue. Respond to one question at a time to ensure the AI adapts correctly to your research context.
*   **Precision and Detail:** The quality of the AI's output is directly proportional to the specificity of the information you provide regarding your methods and data.
*   **Persistence:** For the Socratic Tutor and Skill Creator modules, ensure you save the final generated prompts for future use in your own research environment.
